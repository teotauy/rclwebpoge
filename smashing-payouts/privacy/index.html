Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Overview

Smashing Payouts (redcrowlabs.com/smashing-payouts) is operated by Red Crow Labs. It's a toy: a single page of sliders and charts. There are no accounts, no logins and no forms that send anything anywhere. This policy describes the little there is to describe.

What we collect from you

Nothing. There's no signup, no comment box, no analytics tracker added by us and no cookie set by this site. Moving a slider happens entirely in your browser; none of it is sent to us.

Standard infrastructure logs apply regardless: Vercel, which serves the page, logs requests (IP address, browser type, timestamp) to operate and secure its network. We don't use these logs to identify visitors.

What your browser remembers

The page saves two small things in your own browser's local storage, so it can be a little friendlier next time:

  • Which tab you were last looking at (tour, festival, streams, radio or sync).
  • Whether you've already closed the note from Colby, so it doesn't pop up again.

These never leave your device. Clearing your browser's site data removes them.

Third parties your browser talks to

To draw the page, your browser loads fonts and code directly from these providers, which can see your IP address the same way they would on any site that uses them:

  • Google Fonts: typefaces
  • cdnjs (Cloudflare): the d3 charting library
  • jsDelivr: the d3-sankey diagram library

Links to LinkedIn and Buy Me a Coffee only take you to those sites if you click them.

Not affiliated

Smashing Payouts isn't affiliated with or endorsed by The Smashing Pumpkins, any current or former band member, their management, labels, publishers or any company named on the page.

Changes

This policy may be updated as the site changes. Since nothing is collected, there's nothing to consent to or opt out of; updates will reflect reality as of the date above.
