2. What the data actually is

Fire detections are satellite-derived thermal anomalies from NASA FIRMS (VIIRS), roughly 375m resolution — a detection is not a confirmed, mapped, or contained fire, and heavy cloud cover can hide one entirely. Air quality figures come from the nearest reporting stations in the World Air Quality Index network and may not reflect conditions at your exact location. Wind, humidity, and rainfall are model-derived estimates from Open-Meteo. All of it can be wrong, missing, or hours old — the dashboard states the age of what you're looking at for exactly this reason.
