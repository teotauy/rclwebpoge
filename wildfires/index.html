What this is and isn't

Every number here comes from someone else's instrument. Fire detections are satellite hotspots from NASA FIRMS (VIIRS, near-real-time). A detection is a thermal anomaly at roughly 375m resolution — not a confirmed, mapped, or contained fire — and heavy cloud can hide one entirely. Air quality is World Air Quality Index data from the nearest reporting station, which may sit some distance from the zone itself. Follow the source links before making a call that matters.

Copernicus EMS and Bison Futé publish no stable feed to poll, so they are linked per zone as manual checks rather than pulled automatically.
